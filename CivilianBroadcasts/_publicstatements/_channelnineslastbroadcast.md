# channel nine’s last broadcast

During the activation of SCP-001, multiple broadcast networks were instantly shut down (or not active beforehand). Channel 9, located in North-east Oregon, was airing during the time period indicating SCP-001’s activation.

The Foundation has, to the best of its ability, recovered the active broadcast and analyzed the media. Originally, not much was known from the outside world of what happened to active technology during SCP-001’s activation, and this recovered broadcast has been extremely beneficial in explaining the activation period.

Any anomalous activity present during the broadcast has been logged and recreated for your viewing experience.

## Contents
[23:57] Regular evening programming continues. A special advertisement for Christmas Eve begins.

[23:59] A subtle glitch in televisions occur, silencing the advertisement.

[1:00, beginning of SCP-001’s activation] All televisions flash white for the duration of SCP-001’s activation.

[1:37~] An anomalous broadcast begins, as the original television station is hijacked by an unknown force.

[1:38-2:36] Crude shapes appear as a blur of color and light across televisions in the Channel Nine area. Blotches dance along the screens, causing seizures to epileptic viewers, and large waves of anxiety among the rest.

[2:37~] The anomalous broadcast ends exactly one hour after the beginning. The international EBS then begins to play.
