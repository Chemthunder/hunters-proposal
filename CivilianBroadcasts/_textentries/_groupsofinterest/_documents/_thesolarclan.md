## Overview
**GOI-01**, or “The Solar Clan,” is a large group of civilians who have managed to outwit members of MTF: Zulu-Niner, focused on destruction of all members of MTF: Zulu-Niner in order to “save” the human race.

**GOI-01** works in a tiered hierarchy, with the leader (or the “Sol”) sitting at the top of the food chain. Food is sent from the top down. We have not been able to embed personnel into **GOI-01**.
