## Overview
**GOI-04**, or “Trial by Fire,” is a cultlike organization dedicated to a new religion known as “Sigilism,” or worship of SCP-001-A. Their practices include sacrifices, festivals, and rituals.

**GOI-04** has many public figures (see below), and embedded personnel have observed various new works of art created by followers of Sigilism.

## Objects of Interest
#### The Book of the Faith
A large, inscribed book detailing the practices of Sigilism. Most pages are unknown, save for a few which have been documented, at the expense of embedded personnel. Also known as the “Bible for Sigilists.”
