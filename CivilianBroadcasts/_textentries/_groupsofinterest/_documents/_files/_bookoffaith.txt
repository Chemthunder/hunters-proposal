Page 28 - “In Favor Of-For”

“It was in poor days that the great descended from the heavens, granting all mankind in its light. The dim glow of it gives us hope; spiting those who wish us harm. For those who follow in the Sigil’s flock will be rewarded upon the ending of God's cruel game.”

“We as the flock shall grant the Sigil that which it desires to continue; of flesh and blood. To remain under its protection from that which shall harm.”
