Unknown Voice (UV): Alright. Let’s see if she still works…

*Sound of matches striking.*

UV: It does work!

*The video lights up, revealing a middle-aged man. He’s extremely underweight, a cigarette hanging from his bearded mouth. His hair is wild, and a scar runs down his face, narrowly missing his left eye.*

UV: Hello, whoever reads this. My name is Steven Raines. It’s been… [he clicks his tongue] about 19 days since the Flash?

*The video begins to stabilize, revealing his bloodshot eyes. A small fire lays behind him. He’s in some sort of box, with walls made of stone. Crates of unknown items clutter the space behind him.*

Steven: They told me this thing was dead. I’m lucky I didn’t believe them. Hopefully someone sees this. 

*A small pause.*

Steven: I guess I should explain the situation? 

*Another brief pause.*

Steven: About 19 or so days ago, almost all forms of food creation vanished. Like they never existed. Blipped out of reality, whatever. And that’s not even the most shocking part.

*He pauses to take a breath.*

Steven [In small voice]: Two days after the Flash, these… *things* appeared. Great gray human-lookin’ things. I… I can’t even describe them. Just, look at this.

*Attached is a picture taken by a cellphone camera. The picture is blurry, but it depicts horrifying gray creatures. Enormous wings run along their backs, and long claws extend from their arms. Their eyes light up the photo, a piercing red.*

Steven: They just… started. They killed so many. They came from the ground and just started killing. So many of them. And they kept saying, over and over, “be not afraid, you are expendable.” And then they kept slaughtering, not even wiping the blood from their bodies, and-

*He shivers, as if trying not to cry. He straightens.*

Steven: Moving on. We’ve had to relocate underground just to escape those bastards. What little food we had is running out. 

*He continues, before a screech emanates from a radio somewhere out of view. He panics, and cuts the video abruptly.*
