# Overview
The contents of this recording have been edited by Foundation personnel in order to maintain secrecy over Project [REDACTED], and over **MTF: Zulu-Niner** as a whole. This recording is to be seen by high-ranking personnel only.

Video Date: 19/2/24, twenty-six days following SCP-001’s activation.
## _publicdisturbance.mp4
The camera blips into view, extremely blurry. A recording readout flashes over the bottom of the screen. 

As the camera focuses, the view reveals what was an average city mall, now in disrepair. Cloth banners hang from windows, appearing to obscure them. Doors are barricaded, and shops have been fortified. Hundreds of people swarm around the center fountain, which has been drained. The civilians speak in hushed voices, and move as slowly as possible. It appears as if they are afraid of something.

The camera swivels around, the buzz of the rotations seemingly the loudest thing in the area. 

Almost twenty minutes of this silence passes before a wail begins to build from somewhere in the crowd. Everyone in the mall stops moving, and quietly turns toward a ragged mother, attempting to hush a screaming child. As she tried to comfort it, the child only cried louder. 

A faceless man eventually slams a blanket across the child’s mouth, and looks up at the ceiling. The entire mall seems to hold their breath. A dull tapping sound emanates from the roof, repeating a pattern.

> The Foundation has transcribed this pattern for your viewing experience.
... - .- -. -.. / -... -.--

After what feels like hours, a cutting sound is heard from the ceiling.

Several civilians go wild with fear, shrieking hysterically, begging / praying to [God]. More cutting sounds break through the air, as the roof crumbles in a circular pattern.

Several vaguely humanoid shapes descend from the freshly opened hole, zooming straight towards the civilians. Red flashes from the “heads” of the creatures lit up the camera.

It is at this time that you realize large black redactions have been placed over the creatures. Your instincts howl at you to run from these things. But you stand frozen, looking at the massacre taking place below. 

You see the redactions pass through people, leaving only horribly gutted corpses in their path. At the corner of your view, a flash of light follows a gunshot. The shooter doesn’t seem to particularly aim anywhere, just trying to hit the creatures with a hastily grabbed shotgun. If the bullet(s) hit the beasts, they don’t seem to register it. One leaps directly at the shooter, pulling their head directly off.

You cover your eyes, sinking to your knees, waiting until the screams go silent. After a few minutes (or hours, you lost track of time a while ago), they finally stop. You stand back up, looking at the scene. 

Bodies litter the floors, the fountain being almost filled to the brim with blood and limbs. Gore paints the walls, as if a special effects crew didn’t know when to stop. Gaps in the sea of red dotted the floor at random intervals, seemingly the footprints of those who had done this.

Laying in the center of it all was the child.

Screaming for its mother, who lay mangled in front of it.
