# Department of Cataclysmic Events & Anomalies
Founded in [date], the Department of Cataclysmic Events & Anomalies (DCEA) is a branch of the SCP Foundation designated to the handling and management of world ending, altering, or destructive events. After the discovery of SCP-001, a separate sector was created solely to further studying and research of SCP-001: The 01-Division.

The Department of Cataclysmic Events and Anomalies is managed by three rotating figures, due to the extensive memetic treatment applied to those studying SCP-001.

The Department of Cataclysmic Events and Anomalies manages and surveys all phenomenon, response measures, and protocols related to all known SCP's which may or have caused a scenario resulting in the "end of the world."

Now, the "end of the world" is a very broad term. It could consist of the Earth being unlivable, or all cheese dissapearing.

Here is a list of what the Department of Cataclysmic Events and Anomalies details as "end of the world" scenarios:

- Eradication of all (human or otherwise) life or otherwise.
- Destruction of the Earth.
- Cessation of reality.
- Permenant:
 > Stoppage of time.
 > Death of the human race.
 > Inability to sustain humanity.

We at the SCP Foundation hope to prolong our existence in this reality (or otherwise) for as long as possible!
