# SCP-001-A, "The Sigil"
## Containment Class:
Apollyon

## Containment Method(s):
None.

## Description -
After the activation of **SCP-001**, **SCP-001-A** was observed to appear in the sky, replacing the moon.

It is unknown at this time if exposure to **SCP-001-A** causes any effects, and contact with the object has not been attempted.

**SCP-001-A** appears as a supermassive sigil hanging stationary in the sky. The sigil present appears to change with the cycle of the moon. The symbols shown cannot be deciphered at this time.
