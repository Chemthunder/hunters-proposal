#### _weektwo
“My name is Lusiya Wenslow. I serve as a research assistant at the SCP Foundation. It’s been two weeks since SCP-001 was activated. I am 19 years old.

Sorry, just a formality. The director encouraged us to keep logs (diaries) so we stay sane, since we’re going to be here a while. 

I’ve been generally stationed alone in Site-93, in the eastern Antarctic area. Well, me and two others. They only sent two others so the chances of us going insane went down.

They gave us enough food to last for years it feels like, and there’s never a shortage of water, heh. Pros of living in a literal glacier.

Keeping it short this time since I don’t know how long I'm mandated to write.

Bye!”

#### _weekthree
“My name is Dr. Wenslow. I serve as a research assistant at the SCP Foundation. It’s been three weeks since SCP-001 was activated. I am 19 years old.

I got promoted! They said my studies of SCP-001’s effects on the Antarctic area were enough to justify me being promoted to a full-on researcher!!

Even if it was from another continent, I’m still overjoyed. Dopamine hits are few and far between around here.

Some of the food keeps slipping out from the stores, but none that it’s too much of an emergency… yet.

I’ve been studying a new use for SCP-001-B, hopefully either medicinal or some way of making it into a sustainable food source. I hope it proves to be worth my extensive research.”
