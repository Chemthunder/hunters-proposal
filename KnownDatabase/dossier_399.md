## Overview
Item #: SCP-001-B

Object Class: Cerunnos

Alternatively Known As:
“Landlockets”

### Special Containment Procedures
SCP-001-B can be theoretically contained as any other anomalous flora, but containment is unnecessary due to the object’s non-harmful effects on the environment and civilians as a whole, along with public knowledge of its existence being international.

### Description
SCP-001-B is a new species of vine that began to grow around edible crops of all forms after the activation of SCP-001. SCP-001-B has no effects upon consumption other than not being at all filling, and actively removing useful chemicals and substances from the body. Consumption is possible but needless.

SCP-001-B resembles a bluish-purple vine that coils up along its targets, injecting small thorns into the base of the target. These thorns will inject a form of biological-venom that renders the target inedible, along with being unable to be farmed. This process is almost instantaneous.

SCP-001-B cannot affect humans. Its effects on animals are unknown due to SCP-001.

SCP-001-B, along with most other of SCP-001’s effects on Earth, is known to the public under the name of “Landlockets.”
